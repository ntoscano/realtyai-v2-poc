# Red Planet Urgent Shifts Customer Conversations

## Customer: Taylor McDoogal

Taylor is a new colonist, just arrived on Mars. He started booking shifts with us about a month ago.

_Rough Transcript:_

- [Tell my why you work on our platform]:
  - _Well, I just arrived from Earth and am still trying to land my dream job in the Martian Soccer League. While I’m waiting for tryouts to start, I need cash now and Red Planet lets me pick up shifts and get paid the same day!_
- [What types of shifts do you normally work]:
  - _Oh, I’ll work anything but I’m always looking for the highest pay. I know which places pay the most and I’m always comparing Red Planet with ShiftPlanet. Sometimes, I miss good paying shifts because I’m not checking the apps frequently enough._
- [Do you plan out your schedule in advanced? If so, how far out]:
  - _I’m not a big planner. I like my flexibility and try to keep my schedule open so I’m ready for my big league call-up. I don’t normally pick-up shifts more than a day or two in advance._
  - _My schedule can be a bit chaotic, so sometimes I’ll book a shift, cancel it if a conflict comes up and then finally end-up re-booking it later if I’m my other plans fall through._
- [When you book a shift, how often do you cancel?]:
  - _Oh rarely. I’m a dependable person. There was one time when the subway from Olympus Mons broke down and I missed my shift. I felt terrible, especially because I didn’t have a way to let them know I wasn’t going to make it._

## Customer: Jesse Covington

Jesse is one of our highest earning workers, but also has one of our highest cancellation rates.

_Rough Transcript:_

- [Tell me what you love most about Red Planet]:
  - _The pay. You pay way more than Rust Workers or ShiftPlanet. You also have the most shifts. When I go onto Rust Workers, I hardly find shifts available._
- [How do you decide which shifts to work?]
  - _Again, it’s the pay. I always look for the highest paying options. I check every app every few hours looking for good shifts because they’re hard to find. Even if I’m already booked for that time, I’ll cancel a lower paying shift and book a higher one if I see it._
- [What about worksites, do you have any favorites?]
  - _No, they’re all the same to me. Work is work. I mean, if some have nicer facilities, or treat me kinder, I’ll probably go back to them, but only if the pay makes sense._
- [If you’re running late or can’t make it to a shift, what do you do?]
  - _Well, if I’m running late I still try to make it to the worksite as close to start as possible. If I’ve worked there before I usually have the number of the supervisor and will try to give them a heads up. If it’s a new workplace, then I don’t have a great way to let them know my ETA._
  - _If I can’t make it at all, I’ll cancel the shift and try to let the place know, but that’s not always possible if I don’t have a good way of contacting the worksite. I feel bad when I do this, because I know they’ll be short staffed._
- [At what point do you usually know you’ll be late or unable to work a shift]:
  - _Oh, I know I’m going to be late at least an hour beforehand. It takes at least 45 minutes to get anywhere on this planet. If I have to cancel outright, I usually have a few hours notice, unless there’s a sudden emergency._

## Customer: Felicity Marigold - Shift Supervisor at Deep Space Technologies

Deep Space Technologies is a medium sized customer, posting approximately 10 shifts a week on Red Planet. They joined our platform three months ago.

_Rough Transcript:_

- [Tell me about your experience so far with Red Planet]:
  - _It’s been ok. The quality of your workers is mostly good. A few bad apples, but nothing too serious. Our biggest issues are when shifts don’t get filled, or they get filled but then the worker doesn’t show up._
- [How often does a booked worker not show up]:
  - _Oh, I dunno. Not that often, maybe 15% of the time? But when it happens, it’s a big pain for me because we planned our whole work schedule around that worker showing up. When they don’t, everything gets out of whack and we miss our quota._
    - **[note: I looked at their stats after the call and approximately 90% of their shifts get filled and they’ve only reported 5 shifts out of 120 where a worker didn’t show up]**
  - _I post about the same number of shifts on Rust Workers and never have a worker cancel last minute. If they say someone is coming, I trust them._
- [What else do you like about Rust Workers? Why do you post shifts with them?]:
  - _Corporate just decided a few months ago that we were going to use agencies to fill staff vacancies, so we’ve been trialing both you and Rust Workers. I think you folks have better, more experienced workers than anyone else, but attendance is an issue. I’d rather have a less experienced worker than no worker at all._
  - _Eventually, I’ll have to give my recommendation to corporate, and at this point I’m leaning towards Rust Workers, unless this attendance issue is fixed._
